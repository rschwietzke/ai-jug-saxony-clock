8 /**

10 * The LRU part is not really predictable, but it is fast and simple.
11 * We don't keep a linked list, rather a marker that give every entry a second chance.
12 * If the second chance is false, we can evict this entry. This can happen
13 * rather early. In general, it is still a good enough LRU implementation.
14 * https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/operating-systems/second-chance-or-clock-page-replacement-policy/
15 *
16 * The base hashmap is an open addressing hash table.
